Donate Now

Your gift buys cameras, mounts, and coverage for parks and playgrounds that have none. Give once, or make it monthly, quarterly, or yearly.

Make your gift

Choose your amount and frequency — one-time, monthly, quarterly, or yearly. Every dollar puts cameras where they're needed so kids can play safely.

Donations are processed securely through Donorbox, our donation platform. Recurring gifts (monthly, quarterly, yearly) can be changed or canceled at any time. Trouble with the form above? Open it in a new tab.

Ask your tax professional about the benefits of giving to a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
