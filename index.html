Safe Place To Play (SPTP) is a nonprofit that installs security cameras in parks, playgrounds, courts, and public spaces that don't have them — so families feel safe and kids can be kids.

Our Mission

Why we do this

At Safe Place to Play, our mission is to enhance the safety and security of public recreational spaces by providing video surveillance and CCTV solutions. We are dedicated to creating environments where individuals of all ages—from toddlers to the elderly—can enjoy parks, playgrounds, and communal areas with confidence and peace of mind. By implementing modern security measures, we aim to deter crime, aid in crime prevention, and support efforts to resolve incidents effectively, fostering communities where everyone feels safe to play, relax, and connect.

What We Do

Cameras where there are none

Every project starts with a community that doesn't feel safe — and ends with a space families come back to.

Courts & Fields

Camera coverage for basketball courts, ball fields, and sports complexes — so evening games feel as safe as daytime ones.

Parks & Playgrounds

Eyes on playgrounds, picnic areas, and parking lots — giving parents peace of mind while kids play.

Community Spaces

Walking paths, trails, and gathering places covered — so every corner of the neighborhood feels safe.
