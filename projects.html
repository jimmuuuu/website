Our Projects

Every project below starts with a space people avoided — and ends with cameras watching over it so families come back. These show the kind of camera coverage we bring to public spaces.

Neighborhood Basketball Court

Cameras covering the court, sidelines, and parking area — so evening pickup games feel as safe as afternoon ones.

Youth Baseball Diamond

Coverage for the field, dugouts, bleachers, and lot — so league nights and practices are watched over from first pitch to last.

Community Playground

Cameras watching over the playground, seating, and paths — giving parents peace of mind and kids the freedom to just play.

Community Soccer Field

Full-field coverage including goals, sidelines, and walkways — keeping year-round neighborhood play safe after dark.
