Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

Safe Place To Play (“SPTP,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that installs security cameras in parks, playgrounds, and public spaces. This policy applies to information collected through our website, safeplacetoplay.org.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. That includes:

  • Contact details — such as your name, email address, and phone number, when you submit a project, email us, or reach out through our social media.
  • Project information — details you provide about a location you'd like us to consider, including its address or description.
  • Donation information — when you donate, payment is handled by our secure third-party donation processor. They collect the details needed to process your gift (such as your name, email, and payment information). We do not see or store your full card or bank numbers on this website.

How we use your information

We use the information you share to:

  • Review and respond to project submissions and questions.
  • Process donations and send you a receipt for your records.
  • Keep you updated about our work, events, and impact — only if you've asked us to.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone, ever.

How your information is shared

We only share information with trusted services that help us operate — for example, our donation processor (to complete your gift) and our form/email provider (to deliver messages you send us). These services are bound to protect your information and use it only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share. Donations are processed over secure, encrypted connections by our payment provider. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

Children's privacy

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the personal information you've shared, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Just email us at the address below and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the “last updated” date at the top of this page.
